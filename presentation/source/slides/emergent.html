04 — Philosophy · What security is

System security assessment

Security is an emergent property.

Ideation

Design

Build

Deploy

Operate

It emerges at every stage

Every decision adds or removes some: a design trade, a library pick, a deploy flag, an ops shortcut.

It gets bolted on at every stage

A late design review, a WAF in front of the build, a scanner after deploy, a runbook after the incident.

You can’t assess one stage and call it done. Assess the whole system.
